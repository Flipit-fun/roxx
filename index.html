rETH details
1 ETH = 1 rETH

Swap ETH for rETH, one to one, on Robinhood Chain.

You mint at the live rate, which starts at exactly 1 ETH = 1 rETH, and you can withdraw back to ETH at the current rate anytime. No lock-ups, no waiting.

Target yield

~8% APY · variable

The yield is real rent. The properties behind rETH are leased, and the rent they collect is paid into the vault, so every rETH is worth a little more over time.

Backing

Real property. Real rent.

Every rETH is redeemable for the ETH held in the vault. The yield on top comes from real properties: as rent is paid and property values rise, that gain is returned to the vault. The rate only ever reflects ETH that is actually there — it is variable and not guaranteed.

Operator

Add yield to the vault

Rent and property gains are paid into the vault as ETH. This mints no rETH, so it raises the redemption value of every rETH holder. Anyone can top up; typically only the protocol does.
